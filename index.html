Questions people ask

I can’t find the product I want.
If it isn’t in our catalogue, send us a request and we’ll look for it.
Can I choose the shop my products are bought from?
Yes. We buy the products, or pick up your purchase from the shop you name, and bring them to your door.
Do you sell these products?
No. We don’t sell products as of now. We’re your delivery partner.
Can you run an errand for me?
Possibly. Post, pick-up and drop, and assistance can be done for a small fee. We can run major errands up to Dehradun, but the price may vary.
Why should I book a homestay through you?
We have tie-ups with hotels and homestays, and customers who book through us get a 5% discount.
Will you charge for the carry bag?
If your order is delivered in our company carry bag, Rs 10 is charged. A normal carry bag is free.
Are taxi and scooty services available?
Yes. We only connect you with reliable, high-quality partners we’ve hand-picked for value and a warm welcome.
What other services do you provide?
Ask before you assume. The Request button is there for you to explore.
Who are your collaborating stores?
We work with a wide range of partners. Two of the best known are Omi’s Sweets in Landour and Chacha ka Dhabba in Kulri.
Will the food look exactly like the pictures?
The images give a general view of the product. We don’t promise to deliver exactly what is shown.
How does ordering fruit and vegetables work?
These rules apply to the Fresh category.
1. Inspect: check the quality when the order arrives.
2. Confirm: our delivery partner waits for your confirmation before finalising the drop-off.
3. No returns after delivery: accepting the order is a final sale, with no refunds or returns once delivery is finalised.
Prices of fruit and vegetables may differ from those listed.
Where is the Fine Dine Deluxe restaurant?
Fine Dine Deluxe isn’t a restaurant. It’s an experience where we deliver meals from restaurants with good reviews for the dish you pick.
